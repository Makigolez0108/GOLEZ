About Siargao Island

A beautiful island in Surigao del Norte known for its surf breaks, mangroves, lagoons, and welcoming local communities.

World-Class Surf Breaks

Visit Cloud 9 and discover why surfers from around the world travel to Siargao for its famous waves.

Lush Mangroves & Lagoons

Explore the island's natural beauty, from peaceful lagoons to mangrove forests and clear waters.

Warm Filipino Culture

Experience local traditions, coastal fiestas, Filipino hospitality, and community life.

Traveler Tips

Visitor Guide & FAQ

A few things to consider before your island adventure.

How to Get There?

Check available flights to Sayak Airport (IAO) and arrange transport to your accommodation. Confirm schedules before travelling.

Getting Around

Local tricycles and scooters are common options. Follow road rules, wear a helmet, and confirm fares in advance.

When to Visit?

Weather and surf conditions change throughout the year. Check forecasts and local advisories before activities.

Travel Responsibly

Respect local communities, follow guide instructions, protect wildlife, and take your rubbish with you.
